Elite Residence, Dubai Marina, Dubai

this is a great building

Living in Marina for more than 5 years, I can say that elite is one of the best buildings I have experienced so far.

Elite Residence is a luxurious 91-storey residential building located in the heart of Dubai Marina. It is the fourth tallest skyscraper in Dubai and offers stunning views of the city skyline, the Arabian Gulf, and the Palm Jumeirah.

The building has a wide range of amenities, including a swimming pool, a gym, a sauna and a steam room. There is also a 24-hour security team on duty to ensure the safety of residents.

Overall, I had a very positive experience at Elite Residence and I would definitely recommend it to anyone looking for a luxurious and convenient place to stay in Dubai.

Eliite Residence is one of the best towers in Marina. The building is clean, well maintained with great facilities like indoor and outdoor pool. Apartments are spacious, with open kitchen, dishwasher and all appliances. The only problem I had were noisy neighbours, but that depends on the apartment you get. Being on the shadow part of the tower we didn't have to use AC at all, but we still had to pay bills for it. Overall I recommend Elite Residence if you want to stay in Marina.
