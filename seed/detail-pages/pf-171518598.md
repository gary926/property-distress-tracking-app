Living in Marina for more than 5 years, I can say that elite is one of the best buildings I have experienced so far.

Elite Residence is a luxurious 91-storey residential building located in the heart of Dubai Marina. It is the fourth tallest skyscraper in Dubai and offers stunning views of the city skyline, the Arabian Gulf, and the Palm Jumeirah.
